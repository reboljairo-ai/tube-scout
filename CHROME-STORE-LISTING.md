## DESCRIPCIÓN COMPLETA (pegar tal cual en el formulario)

Stop guessing what content to make. TubeScout gives you the data.

TubeScout is a free research companion for video creators and digital marketers. It helps you find content opportunities, understand what's working in any niche, and make smarter decisions before investing time in a new channel or topic.

---

**WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH TUBESCOUT**

🔍 Niche Research
Type any topic to get an opportunity score (0–100), average views, engagement benchmarks, and top-performing videos in that space. Know instantly whether a niche has room for new creators or is already dominated by established channels.

📈 Trending Content
Discover which videos are gaining traction right now — filtered by format (Shorts, mid-length, long-form) and by region. Useful for spotting emerging topics before they peak.

📺 Channel Research
Enter any channel name or URL to see subscriber count, average views per video, engagement rate, and upload frequency. Great for competitive research or identifying gaps in the market.

🔑 Keyword & Topic Research
Find keyword ideas ranked by search potential and competition level. Based on real video performance data, not guesswork.

✍️ Title Ideas
Generate title variations for any topic based on formats that consistently perform well. Copy with one click.

---

**WHO USES TUBESCOUT**

- Creators researching new content ideas before starting a channel
- Marketers evaluating video content as part of a strategy
- Agencies doing competitive research for clients
- Entrepreneurs validating an idea before committing to a content format

---

**HOW IT WORKS**

TubeScout uses publicly available video and channel data to surface insights directly in a side panel while you browse — no tab switching, no separate dashboard to open.

---

**FREE & PRO PLANS**

Free: 5 analyses per day. Access to niche research, trending content, channel research, and keyword tools.

Pro ($4.99/month): Unlimited analyses per day, full results on every search, search history saved to your account, and saved favorites.

---

**PRIVACY**

TubeScout only accesses pages on the video platform it analyzes. It does not read your email, browsing history, or personal account data. It does not interact with or modify your account in any way.

Full privacy policy: https://tube-scout-production.up.railway.app/privacy
